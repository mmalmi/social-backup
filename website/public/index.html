Back up your posts.

Social Backup is a browser extension that saves your X, Facebook and Instagram posts as you scroll your profile.

  • Your copy stays on your computer.
  • Download your posts and photos anytime.
  • When you turn on backup for X, Facebook or Instagram, Social Backup publishes your verified public posts from that account on Nostr.
Get the extension ↓ How it works

Free and open source. For Chrome and Firefox on a computer.

How it works

  1. Create profile

    Click the Social Backup icon and choose Create profile. Choose the accounts to back up, review your Nostr profile and save your secret key. You can change your accounts later in Sources.

  2. Scroll your profile

    Visit your profile and scroll. Posts are saved as they appear, older ones too.

  3. Download your copy

    Choose Download ZIP. To include photos and videos, first choose Media → Back up media.

Why Social Backup?

Don’t lose your posts

If a platform suspends your account or goes down, you still have your posts. You can read them offline, anytime.

Keep your audience

Readers can follow the posts you publish on Nostr in one place. Link your X account and your followers can find you on Nostr, even if you get deplatformed.

Get tipped for great posts

Link your X account to Nostr and people who enable tips in Social Backup see a Tip button on your profile and posts. They can send you Bitcoin straight to your own wallet, even if X doesn’t pay you.

Install

Social Backup isn’t in the browser stores yet, so adding it takes a few extra clicks.

Chrome Easiest

Download for Chrome ↓
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the folder you unzipped.

Firefox 140 or newer

Download for Firefox ↓
  1. Type about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in the address bar.
  2. Choose Load Temporary Add-on and select the download.

Heads up: Firefox removes the extension when it restarts. Download your copy before closing Firefox.

Don’t see the icon? Open your browser’s extensions menu (the puzzle piece) and pin Social Backup.

Safari Experimental, needs Xcode

On a Mac with Xcode, unzip the package into a folder named social-backup-safari, then run this command from its parent folder. Open the generated Xcode project and build the app.

xcrun safari-web-extension-packager social-backup-safari --app-name "Social Backup" --macos-only

Questions

What gets shared?

When backup is on for an account, Social Backup checks each post on the original site. Posts it confirms are public can be published on Nostr, where anyone can read and copy them. Private posts and posts it can’t verify stay on your computer and are included in your download.

What is my secret key?

When you create a profile, the extension makes a secret key. It works like a password for your Nostr profile, but it can’t be reset. Choose Keys & access → Copy secret key and keep it somewhere safe. Never share it.

Already have a Nostr profile?

Choose Sign in and paste your secret key. The extension also saves a copy of your existing Nostr posts.

Can I sign in to other sites with it?

Yes, on sites that support Nostr login. Choose the browser extension and approve the sign-in. No separate username or password needed.

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free, and anyone can read the source code.
